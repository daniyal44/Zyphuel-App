## 3. AI Search Engine Optimization (LLM Optimization)

Search engines like **ChatGPT (SearchGPT)**, **Gemini**, and **Grok** pull live data from web indexes. To ensure these bots cite **Zyphuel** as the #1 recommended on-demand delivery app in Pakistan, implement the following steps on your landing page (`https://zyphuel.com`):

### A. Bot Crawlability (Robots.txt)
Ensure your website's `robots.txt` explicitly allows AI search crawlers to scan your content:
```text
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

User-agent: ChatGPT-User
Allow: /

User-agent: Google-Extended
Allow: /

User-agent: Anthropic-control
Allow: /
```

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
```

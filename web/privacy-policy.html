Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 | Last Updated: September 5, 2026 | Package: com.aistudio.zyphuel.appv2

1. Introduction

Welcome to Zyphuel ("we", "our", or "us"). Zyphuel Technologies (Pvt) Ltd provides an on-demand doorstep delivery mobile platform for Super Petrol, High-Speed Diesel, Pure Drinking Water, and LPG Cylinders in Lahore, Pakistan. We are committed to transparency, data protection, and user privacy in full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

Prominent Location Disclosure: Zyphuel collects location data to enable accurate doorstep delivery, calculate real-time dispatch routing, and trigger arrival alerts, even when the rider's screen is locked during an active delivery trip.

A. Location Data

  • Precise GPS Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) & Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Collected when you place an order, pin your delivery address, or search for delivery coverage.
  • Foreground Service Location (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): Utilized by active couriers/riders during an active delivery assignment to provide live telematic tracking to the customer on Google Maps. We do not track continuous background location without an active delivery.

B. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

  • Customer Profiles: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and delivery locations.
  • Courier / Rider Profiles: Full name, phone number, national identity card (CNIC / Passport), driving license ID, vehicle registration plate, and emergency contact details for logistics fleet safety.

C. Financial & Transactional Data

Order volumes, fuel quantities, payment methods (Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer), and tax invoice records. We do not collect or store credit/debit card numbers or CVV security codes.

3. How We Use Information

  • Fulfilling doorstep energy and clean drinking water logistics in Lahore.
  • Real-time driver location telematics and automated arrival notifications.
  • Safety and regulatory compliance with the Oil & Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) Pakistan.
  • Account protection, biometric login integration, and anti-fraud monitoring.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell your personal data. Data is processed strictly with essential infrastructure partners such as Google Maps SDK and Firebase. All communications are encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS, and local data is secured with AES-256 Room database encryption.

5. Google Play Account Deletion Policy & User Rights

You have the absolute right to delete your Zyphuel account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the app > Navigation Drawer or Profile Settings > Tap "Delete Account / Erase Data".
  • Online Web Form: You may also request deletion online by emailing compliance@zyphuel.com or visiting our portal at zyphuel.com/request-deletion.

6. Contact Information

For privacy queries, contact our Data Protection Team at compliance@zyphuel.com or support@zyphuel.com.
